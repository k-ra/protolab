# Protolab

running log of experiments in simulating pressings, indentations, print processes, and finishes on digital paper. favorite moments are kept as reusable materials for new work.

[the sheet](https://k-ra.github.io/protolab/protolab/) is the experimental space. [clippings](https://k-ra.github.io/protolab/canvas/) collects them. everything is drawn as a height field and lit in the browser. no build step, so serve the folder with `npx serve .` and run it locally.

## studies

- [cover](https://k-ra.github.io/protolab/): the sheet, with every study resting on it as a paper slip. pick one up, carry it, tap it to open.
- [the sheet](protolab/), [clippings](canvas/), [specimen](flowers/), [the press](press/), [sigil pressure](wax-seal/), [san francisco, round](twin-peaks/)
- [three hands](rosetta/): one decree carved three ways into granodiorite, chalked
- [ebru](ebru/): ottoman marbling, computed exactly with drop and tine transforms
- [sun print](cyanotype/): algae photograms after anna atkins, exposed and washed
- [gilded](gilded/): a raised gold initial on lapis, acanthus in the margins
- [one line](burin/): a single spiral engraving after claude mellan, re-cut as the light moves
- [kintsugi](kintsugi/): a glazed bowl that keeps its breaks in gold
- [smoked drum](kymograph/): a kymograph scratching through soot
- [girih](girih/): hankin strapwork carved in stucco
- [damask](jacquard/): silk woven from punched cards; the flower exists only in the light
- [mirror with a memory](daguerreotype/): a daguerreotype that flips negative in the lamp's reflection
- [sound figures](chladni/): sand on a bowed brass plate
- [morphogenesis](turing/): turing patterns grown as an emboss in paper
- [golden angle](phyllotaxis/): a sunflower head at 137.508°
- [the black manner](mezzotint/): burnish light out of a mezzotint ground
- [camera obscura](obscura/): the world upside down on the far wall; trace it in pencil
- [nature printing](nature-print/): a barnsley fern pressed into lead, printed in green
- [hands on the wall](stencil/): hand stencils blown in ochre on a torchlit cave wall
- [quipu](quipu/): type numbers and they are tied in knotted cord, decimal and positional
- [one stitch](sashiko/): hitomezashi sashiko stitched from the bits of two words
- [rings](rings/): a tree since 1800; hover a ring for its year, the volcanic winters run thin

`lib/gouache.js` paints any height field the way the round map paints terrain.
